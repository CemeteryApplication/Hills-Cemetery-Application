About Us

Dignity, care, and
a home for memories.

Riverside Cemetery of Oswego has been a trusted steward of our community's history since 1852. Nestled in the heart of Oswego County, our grounds offer families a serene and beautifully maintained resting place for those they love.

We are a nonprofit corporation dedicated to the perpetual care of our grounds and the families we serve. Our staff brings compassion and professionalism to every interaction — because we understand that arranging a burial is one of the most significant moments a family will face.

Whether you are planning ahead, navigating a recent loss, or visiting a loved one, we are here to help.

What We Offer

Services for every family's needs.

From traditional burial to pre-planning, we offer a full range of services with transparent pricing and personal attention.

Traditional Burial

Full-service ground burial with a range of lot options, including single and family plots in well-maintained sections throughout our grounds.

Pre-Need Planning

Arrange and pay for burial services in advance — relieving your family of difficult decisions during an already difficult time.

Cremation Interment

Dignified options for the interment of cremated remains, including dedicated garden sections and memorial markers.

Private Mausoleum

Families may purchase a standalone private mausoleum for placement on their lot. Our staff can guide you through available options and placement requirements.

Green Burial

An environmentally conscious option that forgoes embalming and traditional vaults, allowing for a natural return to the earth in a peaceful, maintained setting.

Deed & Records

We maintain thorough records of all lots and interments. Our office can assist with deed transfers, duplicate records, and lot locating.

Monument Placement

We coordinate with monument dealers for the installation of grave markers and memorial stones, following our perpetual care standards.

Perpetual Care

Our grounds are maintained year-round. Perpetual care fees ensure ongoing mowing, landscaping, and upkeep of the entire cemetery.

Page Memorial Chapel

A peaceful space for
gathering & farewell.

Riverside Cemetery is proud to offer the Page Memorial Chapel — a serene, non-denominational space available to families for funeral services, memorial gatherings, and graveside ceremonies.

The chapel provides a dignified, comfortable setting on our grounds, allowing families to hold services in close proximity to the burial site. Whether you are planning a traditional funeral service or a simple gathering of loved ones, the Page Memorial Chapel is available to meet your needs.

To inquire about chapel availability or to schedule a visit, please contact our office directly.

On Our Grounds · Est. 1907

Page Memorial Chapel

The Page Memorial Chapel was donated by the Page family of Oswego in memory of Alanson Sumner Page (1825–1905) and his wife, Elsie Benson Page. Mr. Page was a prominent businessman, civic leader, and four-term mayor of Oswego. Since its completion in 1907, the chapel has been the centerpiece of our cemetery and an important part of the community.

The building is a fine example of stone Gothic architecture. Its squat bell tower and rough-hewn, heavy buttressed walls are offset by intricate tracery in the panel and rose windows — creating an imposing yet graceful effect.

Funeral & Memorial ServicesThe chapel is used frequently for funeral services and memorials, providing families with a dignified and convenient on-site gathering space.

WeddingsThe chapel is also available for weddings, offering a unique and historic stone Gothic setting on the tranquil grounds of Riverside Cemetery.

Stone Gothic ArchitectureBuilt in 1907, the chapel's buttressed stone walls, bell tower, and rose windows make it one of the most distinctive historic structures in Oswego County.
